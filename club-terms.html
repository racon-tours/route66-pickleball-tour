Club Referral Agreement

Version DRAFT-2026-10 · Between Nichols & Company dba Pickle Dot Tours ("Pickle Tours") and the club named at sign-up ("Club").

1. What the Club does

The Club tells its members about the Route 66 Pickleball Tour and shares its members' code and club page. That's it. The Club is a referral partner, not a travel seller.

2. What the Club never does

The Club does not sell, book, quote or operate travel, does not take or hold any guest's money, and does not describe itself as a seller of travel, tour operator or agent of Pickle Tours. All bookings and payments go directly to Pickle Tours.

3. Members' code

Members who book with the Club's code receive the club rate ($500 off per player). Codes do not combine with other offers, and bookings made through third-party travel sites are not eligible.

4. Referral fee

Pickle Tours pays the Club [fee — TBD by counsel] for each paid player seat booked with the Club's code, payable [timing — TBD] after that departure is completed. No fee is owed on cancelled or refunded seats.

5. Names and logos

Each side may use the other's name and logo only to promote the tour and the Club's code, using materials Pickle Tours provides. Pickle Tours hosts the Club's page at route66pickleballtour.com and may update or remove it.

6. Ending the arrangement

Either side may end this agreement at any time with written notice (email is fine). Fees already earned on completed departures are still paid.
