Club Referral Agreement

Version 2026-11-12 · Between Nichols & Company dba Pickle Dot Tours, 4303 McPherson Ave, St. Louis MO 63108 ("we", "us") and the club named at sign-up ("Club", "you").

The short version. Your members save $500 per person on the Route 66 Pickleball Tour with your club's code. For every booking made with the code, the club earns $500 once the guests travel. We handle all bookings and payments. You never sell the tour or touch anyone's money.

1. What the Club does

The Club tells its members about the Route 66 Pickleball Tour and shares its members' code and club link. The Club is a referral partner only.

2. What the Club never does

  • Sell, book, quote, price or operate travel, or take, hold or pass on any guest's money.
  • Describe itself as a seller of travel, tour operator, travel agent or agent of Pickle Dot Tours.
  • Make promises about the tour beyond what is in the materials we provide. The only price-related statement the Club makes is the members' discount ($500 off per person).
  • Post its code on coupon, deal or cash-back sites, or buy search ads on our names ("Pickle Dot Tours", "Route 66 Pickleball Tour").

3. Members' code

Guests who enter the Club's code when they book save $500 per person. The code must be entered at booking; it can't be added afterward. One code per booking, and it doesn't combine with other offers, including the travel-advisor price. Bookings made through a travel advisor or a third-party travel site are not eligible.

4. Referral fee

  • Earned on each Qualifying Booking: a booking made with the Club's code that is paid in full and on which at least one guest takes the trip.
  • $500 per Qualifying Booking, however many guests are on it.
  • No fee on bookings that are cancelled or refunded in full, on which no guest travels, or that are made up only of complimentary seats.
  • Paid by ACH or check within 30 days after the booking's departure ends. We need a completed IRS Form W-9 before the first payment, and we issue a Form 1099 where the law requires it. The Club is responsible for its own taxes.
  • Our booking records decide which bookings used the Club's code. Each booking is credited to one partner only.

5. Telling members about the fee

Because the Club earns a fee, it tells members so when it promotes the tour. A line like "Our club earns a referral fee when you book with our code" is enough.

6. Club page and member sign-ups

We host the Club's page at route66pickleballtour.com and may update or take it down. People who sign up on that page join our mailing list and are handled under our Privacy Policy. We may tell the Club how many sign-ups and bookings came through its code.

7. Names and logos

Each side may use the other's name and logo only to promote the tour and the Club's code, using materials we provide or approve. Neither side gains any other rights in the other's name, logo or content.

8. Independent parties

The Club and Pickle Dot Tours are independent. Nothing here makes either side the other's agent, employee, partner or joint venturer, and neither can bind the other. The Club has no role in running the tour and no responsibility for it.

9. Liability

Pickle Dot Tours alone is responsible for the tour and for its dealings with guests. Each side is responsible for its own statements and conduct. Our total liability to the Club under this agreement is limited to referral fees earned and unpaid, and neither side is liable to the other for indirect or lost-profit damages.

10. Changes and ending

We may change these terms with 30 days' notice by email; changes apply to bookings made after they take effect. Either side may end this agreement at any time by email. Fees on Qualifying Bookings made before it ends are still paid after those guests travel. We may end it immediately, and withhold fees on the affected bookings, if the Club breaks section 2.

11. Everything else

Missouri law governs this agreement, and any dispute is heard in the state or federal courts in St. Louis, Missouri. This is the whole agreement about the referral program. Agreeing online at sign-up is a valid signature, and the person who agrees confirms they are authorized to act for the Club.
