St. Louis

Missouri

Welcome to our neighborhood — Mike and Trina live a few blocks away. The Central West End is the city's most walkable dining district, and Forest Park, one of the largest city parks in the country, is across the street.

Your hotel

Royal Sonesta Chase Park Plaza

212 N. Kingshighway Blvd., Central West End

A 1920s landmark across the street from Forest Park, with its own cinema, steakhouse and pool-side bar.

Inside the hotel

The Tenderloin Room

In the hotel

A St. Louis steakhouse institution, all dark wood and white tablecloths.

Chase Club

In the hotel

Gastropub menu, a big bar and a patio by the pool with an outdoor fireplace.

Chase Park Plaza Cinemas

In the hotel

A real movie theater downstairs. Catch a film after dinner without stepping outside.

Dinner nearby

Brasserie by Niche

4580 Laclede Ave. — about 5 minutes on foot

A French bistro from one of the city's best chefs: steak frites, mussels, roast chicken.

Bar Italia

13 Maryland Plaza — about 5 minutes on foot

A neighborhood Italian fixture with a big sidewalk patio.

Vicia

Cortex district — a short ride

Vegetable-forward tasting menus, recognized nationally. Worth a reservation.

A Route 66 detour

Ted Drewes Frozen Custard

6726 Chippewa St. — about 15 minutes by car

On old Route 66 since the 1940s. Order a "concrete" — it's served upside down to prove how thick it is.

See and do

Forest Park

Across the street

Home of the 1904 World's Fair. The Saint Louis Art Museum, the Zoo and the Missouri History Museum are all inside and free to enter.

Cathedral Basilica of Saint Louis

4431 Lindell Blvd. — about 10 minutes on foot

One of the largest mosaic collections in the world, covering the walls and domes.

World Chess Hall of Fame

4652 Maryland Ave. — about 5 minutes on foot

Look for the giant king on the sidewalk out front.

Euclid Avenue and Maryland Plaza

Steps from the hotel

Shops, sidewalk cafés and bars for an easy evening stroll.

Good to know

  • Westbound is here on a Monday, when some kitchens close. Check before you walk over.
  • Forest Park is beautiful but big. Use a rideshare to reach the museums if you're short on time.
