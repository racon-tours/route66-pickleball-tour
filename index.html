The Idea

A tournament you travel through.

Most pickleball trips are a resort with courts attached. This is different: the tournament is the trip. You board in Chicago with 31 other players, and by the time you step off at the Pacific, you've played your way across eight states — seeded, bracketed, and crowned along the way.

Blind doubles means you don't bring a partner; the road assigns you one. Early rounds sort the field, the bracket decides it, and the back half of the trip belongs to open play, coaching sessions with local club pros, and the roadside America you've only ever flown over.

Nobody goes home a loser. Everybody goes home with a story.

The Tournament

Blind doubles. Real stakes. No sad exits.

You register alone. Partners are assigned, and the opening days on the road double as seeding rounds — every match counts toward your bracket position.

The championship is decided over a full weekend at Tournament HQ on Grand Lake: twelve purpose-built courts, one 16-team bracket, and a consolation draw that keeps every pair playing meaningful matches to the end.

After the bracket, the paddle doesn't go away. The back half of the route runs optional singles, pickup doubles, and coaching sessions with local club pros in every city.

  • Days 1–3
    Seeding roundsRound-robin play in Chicago, St. Louis, and Grand Lake sets the bracket.
  • Days 4–5
    Championship weekend16-team double-draw bracket at Tournament HQ. Finals under the lights.
  • Days 6–9
    The open roadOptional singles, pickup doubles, and pro-led clinics city by city.
  • Day 10
    End of the trailAwards at the Pacific. Every player finishes with a record — and a road trip.
