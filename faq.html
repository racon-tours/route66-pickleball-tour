Before you ask.

The pickleball

What level do I need to be?

Beginner to 4.0+. There's no rating gate and no minimum. We ask for your DUPR (or your best guess) when you book, but it doesn't set your seed: the directors seed the bracket from watching everyone play at the Day 2 session.

Do I need to bring a partner?

No. Come alone and the draw deals you in. Come as a pair and the doubles bracket is yours together, start to finish. Blind doubles mixes everyone up either way.

Do I have to play every session?

Never. Play is always optional, and you decide when to sit one out. Every session is a morning session, so afternoons belong to the road either way.

How many sessions are there, and where?

Ten, all in the mornings, on courts booked for the tour alone: club sessions with local pros along the way, and three sessions at each of the two resorts. See the westbound and eastbound day-by-day pages.

What about altitude?

We never schedule play above about 6,000 feet, so Flagstaff is a sightseeing stop, not a court stop. Albuquerque sits at about 5,300 feet, so that session is a shorter 90-minute block.

What happens if I'm knocked out of the bracket?

You keep playing. Every session has clinics with local pros and rec play, and nobody's tour ends in elimination.

Who's coming

How big is the group?

Thirty-two players per departure, plus just a few non-playing companions, and your tour directors, Mike and Trina, who ride every departure.

Can a non-player come along?

Yes. There are just a few non-playing companion seats on each departure. Companions ride, stay, eat and celebrate with the group; they just skip the draw.

Is there a minimum age?

Every guest must be 21 or older.

I'm traveling solo. Will you match me with a roommate?

We don't match roommates. Solo travelers book a single room, or bring a friend and share.

What's included

What does the price include?

The motorcoach, ten nights of hotels, all ten sessions, club-pro coaching, breakfast and lunch every day, the Kickoff Reception and the Farewell Happy Hour.

Are dinners included?

No, and that's on purpose. Every other evening you're loose in some of the best roadfood country in America. Each overnight has a guide to the neighborhood around the hotel — start with Chicago.

What are the hotels like?

Every night is in a hotel with a story: an 1893 athletic club in Chicago, a lakeside resort under a court dome in Oklahoma, a restored 1926 railway hospital in Albuquerque, the desert's first resort in La Quinta, and Art Deco on the ocean in Santa Monica.

What's the motorcoach like?

A full-size luxury motorcoach with a restroom on board and room to spread out.

Booking and payment

How much is the deposit?

$500 per person holds your seat. It's fully refundable for 3 days after you book; after that it's non-refundable.

When is the balance due?

75 days before departure. Inside 60 days, payments are non-refundable.

What if I can't go?

You can hand your seat to someone else up to 45 days before departure. Full details are in our booking terms.

Should I buy travel insurance?

We recommend it, though it isn't required. A policy that covers trip cancellation and medical care away from home is the one to look for.

Getting there and home

Where does the tour start and finish?

It's a one-way tour. Westbound starts in Chicago and finishes in Santa Monica; eastbound runs the other way. Fly into one end and home from the other.

Which airports should I use?

Chicago O'Hare or Midway at the Chicago end, and LAX at the Santa Monica end.

When should I arrive?

Day 1 is arrival day, with the Kickoff Reception at the hotel from 6:00 to 8:00 pm. Plan to land that afternoon. The motorcoach leaves on the morning of Day 2.

When can I fly home?

The Farewell Happy Hour is on the last evening, and your final hotel night is included, so book your flight home for Day 11. Want a few extra days at either end? Tell us and we'll help you add nights.

How much luggage can I bring?

A normal suitcase plus your paddle bag. The motorcoach has full-size luggage bays, and baggage handling at every hotel is covered.
